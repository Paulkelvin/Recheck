# User research: YouTube comment findings

A running log of noteworthy findings from harvesting comments on Nigerian
land/survey YouTube videos, kept for pattern-spotting before acting on any
single one. Append new batches under a new dated heading rather than
editing old entries — the point is to watch what recurs across multiple
sources, not just react to one video's comment section.

Each entry: what was said (paraphrased, no need to keep full usernames),
why it matters to Recheck, and a status once it's actually been acted on.

---

## Batch 1 — 2026-08-16

Source videos (by YouTube ID, from the harvested files): `3umGeESHbIE`,
`M1tdltUUZVY`, `HYZc8nooBYM`. ~109 comments total, most were pleasantries
("thank you sir") — signal below is the ~8 substantive ones.

### Worth researching now

**Lagos State has an official online barcode verification system.**
A commenter explained: since roughly a year before this comment, Lagos
State issues a "Certificate of Deposit" with a barcode, verifiable online,
once a survey is registered with the Surveyor General's office (~60 days
after lodging). Neither the asker nor apparently many buyers know this
exists. This is a real, independent, government-run verification channel —
worth finding the actual portal and figuring out whether Recheck can
cross-reference it or at minimum point users to it. Likely the strongest
authenticity signal available for Lagos plans, stronger than anything OCR
can determine on its own.
**Status:** not yet investigated.

### A confusion worth productizing

**"Provisional" vs "registered" survey.** A buyer had a provisional survey
handed to them by a real estate company, didn't know it can't be used for
building-plan approval, and was frustrated the seller hadn't explained the
limitation. Recurring enough to suggest our plan-authenticity check should
explicitly detect and call out "this looks provisional, not registered" as
its own distinct finding, rather than folding it into a generic pass/fail.
**Status:** not yet built.

### Marketing/copy ammunition

**Some buyers don't believe a "registered" plan can be forged at all.** A
real exchange: one commenter insisted a registered survey can't be
doctored; another replied "when you fall victim, you'll know better."
Copy should directly address this — not just "is this plan real" but "yes,
even registered plans get forged, here's how."

### UX/trust lesson (not a feature)

On an unrelated tool's video: *"Why are you requesting people's data
before anyone can access the website?"* — real friction around forms
asking for personal info before showing any value. Worth checking whether
Recheck's own flow asks for login/signup before showing anything, versus
showing value (e.g. the free plot preview) first.

### Smaller data points

- Backdating survey plans is a scam pattern common enough that the
  National Institute of Surveyors has explicitly banned it.
- A buyer asked what to actually do when a plan shows "agricultural
  acquisition" status. Our government-acquisition check currently returns
  pass/fail/flagged without next steps — an opening to add actionable
  guidance (e.g. "apply through NTDA for allocation") rather than a bare
  verdict.
- Multiple people asked "how do I verify a survey plan online" and "what
  software do you use to verify coordinates" with no self-service answer
  given beyond "ask a surveyor" — direct validation that the market gap
  Recheck fills is real and currently unaddressed, but also a reminder
  that awareness of Recheck itself is the actual bottleneck, not belief
  that the problem exists.
